    What's happening?

    Press START. The Producer creates baggage and places it on the conveyor. Consumers take it off and sort it.

    Airport Baggage Flow · Producer–Consumer problem · bounded buffer, semaphores & mutex. Frontend only: HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript.

    🎓 Viva mode

    What is the Producer?

    The check-in system that generates baggage.

    What is the Consumer?

    The sorting system that processes baggage.

    What is the Buffer?

    Temporary storage between Producer and Consumer: the baggage conveyor, with a fixed capacity.

    What happens when the buffer is full?

    The Producer must wait until a Consumer removes a bag.

    What happens when the buffer is empty?

    Consumers must wait until the Producer adds a bag.

    What is a Race Condition?

    When multiple processes access shared data simultaneously and the result can become incorrect.

    Why Mutex?

    To allow only one process to access the critical section at a time.

    Why Semaphore?

    To control access to available and occupied buffer slots: empty counts free slots, full counts filled slots.

    Producer:                Consumer:
  wait(empty)              wait(full)
  insert(bag)              wait(mutex)
  signal(full)             remove(bag)    // critical section
